\section{Discussion and Limitations}
The proposed design links molecular construction to a testable localization phenotype. Its main limitation is that overexpression and fluorescent tagging can influence TFEB regulation. Fusion orientation therefore requires explicit justification, and a reporter response should not be equated with a complete description of endogenous TFEB behavior.

The localization assay examines redistribution rather than autophagic flux or lysosomal function directly. Changes in nuclear localization alone will not establish increased autophagic degradation. Follow-up studies could compare reporter localization with endogenous TFEB or test selected downstream targets. These extensions are outside the initial cloning-and-localization objective.
