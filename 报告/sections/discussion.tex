\section{Discussion and Scope}
The design links construct identity, nutrient-sensitive distribution, selected regulatory sites and downstream function. Its strength comes from comparisons that separate competing explanations: matched nutrient/solvent controls, endogenous confirmation, expression titration, individual and combined site substitutions, genetic perturbation, and loss-and-rescue. Execution runs from construct acceptance through the cell-assay pilot and localization/recovery confirmation to the mechanism and function modules, and each stage resolves its quality checks before the next begins.

Each control answers a defined question. EGFP-only reports vector and fluorescent-protein effects, and endogenous TFEB measurements test whether the fusion behaves like the native protein. Site substitutions change local chemistry together with phosphorylation potential. RPTOR/RICTOR comparisons attribute the response to an mTOR complex when knockdown is competent, and a separate biochemical experiment is needed to show direct phosphorylation. Recovery of N/C measures net redistribution. TFEB knockdown and rescue establish a TFEB contribution, and the TFE3 controls address the other MiT/TFE-family mediators.

Nuclear accumulation, transcription and autophagic turnover are distinct endpoints. A stress response can move TFEB into nuclei without improving degradation, and drug-induced autophagy also involves TFEB-independent pathways. The functional conclusions therefore rest on their own genetic and inhibitor controls, and when assays disagree the conclusion narrows to the supported level.

The C-terminal extension adds a question beyond reproducing redistribution. Its factorial comparison estimates genotype--fusion-architecture--stimulus effects, and the primary RNA endpoint is the total effect in a calibrated expression system. Removing both linker and EGFP isolates the fusion architecture, and the linker-only constructs of R06 split an architecture effect into its linker and EGFP components. Alanine and threonine substitutions change local chemistry together with phosphorylation, which the 3T arm and the phosphosite assay separate. Nuclear abundance and localization are mediators, and a qualified nascent-RNA assay, TFE3 controls and matched nuclear exposure support an intrinsic-activity interpretation. Phosphosite and ChIP measurements carry their own specificity and recovery requirements. The primary contrast can be zero when both drugs share an effect, so component contrasts are always reported, and a null result with a wide interval reports the precision achieved.

The conclusions apply to TFEB isoform 1, the calibrated expression range, HeLa cells and the stated drug regimens. HeLa cells lack LKB1 \cite{Fogarty2010}, so AMPK-related findings refer to that background. A key contrast is repeated in a second human line that expresses LKB1, such as HEK293 or U2OS, with its own expression and pathway qualification before any generalization. The research extension raises the workload well beyond an 18-condition screen, and the calendar yields to assay qualification and the fixed precision-based sample size.

This is an experimental proposal, and no wet-lab clones, images, blots, qPCR measurements or flux data have been acquired. The sequence files define the intended mutant identities. Supplier-specific reagents, siRNA targets, qPCR oligos, antibody performance and final doses/sample size are fixed after the documented pilot validation. These implementation items and the authors' actual contributions are completed before execution or course submission.
