\subsection{Sequence-Defined Construct and Materials}
The proposed construct, \ConstructName{}, expresses human TFEB followed by a C-terminal EGFP tag under the CMV promoter. The reference is TFEB transcript variant 1, NM\_007162.3, whose CDS at transcript positions 303--1733 encodes NP\_009093.1 (\TFEBAa{} amino acids). A native, intron-free \TFEBReferenceNt{}-nt CDS containing its original \oligo{TGA} is ordered as linear double-stranded DNA without codon optimization, for example through the gBlocks HiFi synthesis service. This defined template fixes the TFEB isoform at the outset. Supplier QC and the delivered sequence are checked on receipt~\cite{TFEBRef,IDTTemplate}.

The empty backbone is pEGFP-N1, GenBank U55762.1 (\EmptyVectorBp{} bp). Kanamycin resistance selects in \textit{E. coli}; neomycin/G418 resistance is used only for mammalian selection. The reference map includes the CMV promoter, EGFP, polyadenylation signals and replication origins~\cite{VectorRef,VectorManual}. C-terminal EGFP has a precedent in TFEB localization experiments: Addgene \#38119 (pEGFP-N1-TFEB) was built with HindIII/KpnI, whereas the present design uses HindIII/BamHI~\cite{RoczniakFerguson2012,Addgene38119}.

The accepted \textit{in silico} design is \ConstructBp{} bp (Figure~\ref{fig:plasmid}). It retains the native TFEB start codon, removes the TFEB stop through reverse-primer placement, and connects TFEB to EGFP through the seven-residue \oligo{\LinkerPeptide{}} linker. EGFP's \oligo{ATG} remains an internal methionine codon, and its terminal \oligo{TAA} ends the \FusionAa{}-residue fusion. The forward primer adds \oligo{ACC} immediately before TFEB's start, giving an A at position $-3$ and G at $+4$. Reverse-primer \oligo{CC} contributes coding-strand \oligo{GG} to preserve the reading frame. Both junctions and the entire fusion were checked computationally.

The laboratory workflow (Figure~\ref{fig:workflow}) uses Q5 High-Fidelity $2\times$ Master Mix (NEB M0492) for cloning PCR, HindIII-HF R3104 and BamHI-HF R3136 in rCutSmart, Monarch cleanup and gel-extraction kits, T4 DNA Ligase M0202, NEB 5-alpha C2987 competent \textit{E. coli} (a DH5$\alpha$ derivative), Monarch T1110 minipreps and Sanger primer walking. OneTaq M0482 is used only for screening, and its products do not enter the construct.

\subsection{Product Assessment, Restriction Digestion and Ligation}
A 5 $\mu$L PCR aliquot is analyzed on 1.0\% agarose in $1\times$ TAE with $1\times$ loading dye, a compatible DNA stain and 0.5 $\mu$g NEB N3232 1 kb ladder. Electrophoresis starts at 5 V/cm of electrode separation and stops after the relevant bands separate and before the tracking front exits. The ladder's 500/517 bp pair co-migrates. UV exposure of DNA intended for cloning is minimized~\cite{MethodM16}.

A single \PCRBp{}-bp PCR product is purified with Monarch Spin PCR \& DNA Cleanup T1130: 20--100 $\mu$L sample, five sample volumes of prepared BZ, an S1A column, two 200 $\mu$L WZ washes, and 20 $\mu$L EY elution after a 1 min wait. Centrifugation follows the current kit protocol at $16{,}000\times g$ and room temperature. If additional bands occur, the target band is excised and purified with T1120 using four gel-slice volumes of BY, dissolution near $50\,^{\circ}$C, an S1A column, two 200 $\mu$L WZ washes and 20 $\mu$L EY elution. Alcohol-containing buffers are prepared according to their current bottle labels. Recovered DNA is quantified and used at its measured concentration~\cite{MethodM09,MethodM10}.

PCR product and empty vector are digested separately with the common recipe in Table~\ref{tab:digest-ligation}, at $37\,^{\circ}$C for 60 min. Both enzymes have full listed activity in rCutSmart, and the enzyme volumes contribute 2\% glycerol. Actual DNA and water volumes are recalculated from measured concentrations; insufficient material prompts a repeat or a scaled preparation. Uncut and single-digest analytical controls accompany troubleshooting of incomplete digestion. BamHI-HF cannot be heat-inactivated, so digestion products are purified before ligation~\cite{MethodM02,MethodM05,MethodM19}.

Unpurified BamHI-HF digests for direct preparative or diagnostic gel loading receive SDS-containing Purple Gel Loading Dye B7024 ($6\times$), diluted to $1\times$ by adding one volume of dye to five volumes of sample; the SDS dissociates residual enzyme-bound DNA, and the no-SDS B7025 product is not used. Dye is added only to gel samples. If SYBR Safe or GelRed is used, staining follows electrophoresis as directed for B7024~\cite{MethodM19,MethodM20}.

The \BackboneBp{}-bp backbone is gel-purified to separate residual circular vector and the excised \RemovedMCSBp{}-nt MCS. The digested insert is purified with T1130. Its nominal retained length is \InsertBp{} nt; the discarded top-strand terminal pieces are 7 and 11 nt. These pieces and the 38-nt MCS run below the resolving range of the gel. Both retained fragments have equal strand lengths; their pydna duplex spans are 4,699 and 1,443 bases because of staggered ends. Gel sizes use the nominal top-strand cut-to-cut lengths, and molarity uses mean strand lengths, which equal those nominal lengths for the selected ends. The distinct 5$'$ \oligo{AGCT} and \oligo{GATC} overhangs support directional ligation, so dephosphorylation is omitted; the vector-only ligation reports incomplete digestion or circular-vector carryover.

The initial ligation contains 20 fmol backbone and 60 fmol insert (3:1 insert:vector). With mean strand length $L$, the mass conversion is
\[
m\;\mathrm{(ng)}=n\;\mathrm{(fmol)}\times L\;\mathrm{(nt)}\times0.00066.
\]
The calculated inputs are 61.974 ng backbone and 56.9844 ng insert. Table~\ref{tab:digest-ligation} gives a practical recipe rounded to 0.01 $\mu$L. The unrounded total DNA concentration is 5.948 ng/$\mu$L, within the manufacturer's 1--10 ng/$\mu$L starting range. Fully thawed ATP-containing ligase buffer is mixed before use. Ligation proceeds at $20$--$25\,^{\circ}$C for 10 min, followed by $65\,^{\circ}$C for 10 min to stop the reaction. A matched vector-only ligation replaces the insert volume with water. The restriction-cut ends carry 5$'$ phosphates, so no polynucleotide kinase step is needed~\cite{MethodM06}.

\subsection{Transformation, Screening and Plasmid Preparation}
For each transformation, 2 $\mu$L ligation is added to 50 $\mu$L NEB 5-alpha C2987 cells. Cells remain on ice for 30 min, receive heat shock at $42\,^{\circ}$C for 30 s, return to ice for 5 min, and receive 950 $\mu$L SOC. Recovery proceeds at $37\,^{\circ}$C and 250 rpm for 60 min. Then 100 $\mu$L each of undiluted recovery and 1:10 and 1:100 SOC dilutions are plated on LB-kanamycin and incubated at $37\,^{\circ}$C overnight. Kanamycin is 30 $\mu$g/mL in bacterial plates and liquid cultures; G418 is reserved for mammalian cells~\cite{MethodM07,MethodM08}.

Vector-only ligation, a no-DNA control and the supplied pUC19 transformation control are processed in parallel. pUC19 is selected on ampicillin at 100 $\mu$g/mL. These controls report backbone background, contamination or selection failure, and competent-cell efficiency, respectively~\cite{MethodM08}.

Eight isolated colonies are screened initially while numbered patches are retained. Each 25 $\mu$L OneTaq M0482 reaction receives trace colony material, 12.5 $\mu$L $2\times$ mix, 0.5 $\mu$L of each 10 $\mu$M diagnostic primer and 11.5 $\mu$L water; final primer concentrations are 0.2 $\mu$M. Colony lysis is $94\,^{\circ}$C for 5 min, followed by 30 cycles of $94\,^{\circ}$C for 30 s, $58\,^{\circ}$C for 30 s and $68\,^{\circ}$C for 160 s, then $68\,^{\circ}$C for 5 min. The 160 s extension covers the longest modeled 2,608-bp product at 1 min/kb or longer. Annealing starts at $58\,^{\circ}$C, with a $55/58/61\,^{\circ}$C gradient if needed~\cite{MethodM12}.

S-F1/S-R1 flank the insert, while S-F2/S-R1 pair an internal TFEB primer with a downstream vector primer to test orientation (sequences and sites in Table~\ref{tab:sanger}). Their model $T_m$ values are $60.04/62.55\,^{\circ}$C and $66.09/62.55\,^{\circ}$C, respectively, using 50 mM monovalent ions, 1.8 mM Mg$^{2+}$, 0.8 mM total dNTP and 200 nM primer~\cite{Primer3API}. The correct construct predicts 2,605 bp for flanking PCR and 2,093 bp for orientation PCR; an empty vector predicts 1,204 bp for flanking PCR and no orientation product. Empty-vector and no-template controls accompany screening. In the first construction round, flanking-positive minipreps proceed to sequencing even if every orientation assay is negative; a sequence-accepted clone then serves as the orientation positive control in later rounds.

Up to three screening-positive colonies are grown independently in 3 mL LB-kanamycin at $37\,^{\circ}$C for 12--16 h with aeration; if fewer are recovered, those available are analyzed. For each T1110 miniprep, 1.5 mL culture is processed with 200 $\mu$L RNase-containing B1, 200 $\mu$L B2 for 1 min with gentle inversion, then 400 $\mu$L B3 for 2 min. After clarification, DNA binds to an S2D column, receives 200 $\mu$L BZ and 400 $\mu$L WZ washes, and elutes in at least 30 $\mu$L EY. The manufacturer's additional 5 min BZ incubation is used for DNA intended for transfection. Yield and purity are measured~\cite{MethodM11,MethodM18}.

A 500-ng miniprep aliquot undergoes the defined 50-$\mu$L HindIII/BamHI digestion and gel analysis with the B7024 handling above. Agreement with \BackboneBp{}- and \InsertBp{}-bp fragments confirms the insert and backbone sizes. Insert orientation, short indels, substitutions and the stop codon are resolved by sequencing, which is the acceptance gate.

\paragraph{Transfection-grade preparation.} Each sequence-accepted clone is scaled from its glycerol stock to a midi-scale endotoxin-removal plasmid preparation for all cell experiments, with the kit and supplier fixed at procurement. Each lot receives A260/A280 and A260/A230 readings, a HindIII/BamHI diagnostic digest and Sanger reads across both junctions. Each construct has two independent lots, one from each accepted clone, which alternate across experimental days; lot numbers are recorded with every experiment so that no genotype is tied to a single preparation batch.

\subsection{Sanger Primer Walking and Acceptance Criteria}
Each candidate is sequenced with the ten primers in Table~\ref{tab:sanger}, in separate reactions. For Eurofins Standard Read purified-plasmid submissions, this construct falls in the 150--250 ng/$\mu$L stock range. A target of 200 ng/$\mu$L and 55 $\mu$L DNA per clone supports ten reads in the separate-template format ($10+9\times5$ $\mu$L). If needed, independently prepared aliquots from the same single-colony culture are pooled and measured or concentrated; different clones are never pooled. Each primer is supplied separately at 10 $\mu$M, with at least 10 $\mu$L for its first read and 5 $\mu$L per additional use. Current service requirements are rechecked before ordering~\cite{MethodM13}.

Sanger-primer estimates use Primer3/SantaLucia with 50 mM monovalent ions, no added Mg$^{2+}$ or dNTP, and 50 nM oligo. Selection required 18--25 nt, 35--60\% GC, $T_m$ of $50$--$62\,^{\circ}$C, no four-base homopolymer, a terminal G/C without three consecutive terminal G/C bases, and modeled hairpin/homodimer $T_m<40\,^{\circ}$C. Each oligo has one exact full-length binding site in the final construct~\cite{Primer3API,MethodM13}.

The planning window comprises extended bases 51--650 after each primer's 3$'$ end (600 usable bases), which excludes the early low-quality region and stays within a standard-read length. Adjacent planned windows overlap by at least 50 nt. The acceptance target is positions 602--2799 (2,198 nt): 20 upstream vector bases, the 5$'$ junction and initiation context, and the full \FusionORFNt{}-nt fusion ORF through its final stop. The computational coverage check gives every target base at least one forward and one reverse read, with primer-binding bases covered by other primers. The outer S-F1 and S-R1 sites lie outside the target, and sequence beyond it derives from the unmodified reference vector~\cite{MethodM13,MethodM14}.

A clone is accepted when actual quality-trimmed chromatograms give unambiguous, concordant Q20-or-better calls in both directions across the entire target, no unexpected nucleotide changes, and the predicted restriction pattern. Reads are aligned to the exact B-v1 reference, with manual inspection of junctions and all mismatches~\cite{MethodM15}. Low-quality regions, single-strand gaps and ambiguous peaks trigger additional reads or primers. Reproducible mixed peaks prompt single-colony re-isolation and a fresh preparation. A clear unwanted coding or junction mutation rejects that clone; a shared error across independent clones prompts investigation of the template and design.

\subsection{Troubleshooting}
No cloning-PCR band prompts checks of template identity, dilution, primer matching and annealing conditions; additional bands prompt the gradient and target-band purification. High vector-only background prompts checks for incomplete digestion and residual circular DNA. Absence of colonies in both the test and positive transformation control points to handling or selection problems before a failed ligation is inferred. An apparently correct gel pattern with sequence disagreement fails acceptance. Only sequence-accepted plasmid enters the HeLa experiments.
