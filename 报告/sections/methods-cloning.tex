\subsection{Construct Strategy and Materials}
The selected reporter is wild-type human TFEB isoform 1 fused to C-terminal EGFP (TFEB--EGFP), expressed from pEGFP-N1 under the CMV promoter. This architecture follows the HeLa localization study of Roczniak-Ferguson et al. and its deposited construct, pEGFP-N1-TFEB (Addgene \#38119) \cite{RoczniakFerguson2012,Addgene38119}. It replaces the initial outline's provisional pEGFP-C1 strategy. The choice follows a directly relevant experimental precedent; it does not establish that fluorescent tagging leaves endogenous TFEB regulation unchanged.

The TFEB reference is RefSeq NM\_007162.3, encoding NP\_009093.1 (476 amino acids). Its CDS occupies transcript positions 303--1733 (1-based, inclusive): 1,431 nucleotides including the terminal TGA, or 1,428 nucleotides without that stop codon \cite{TFEBRefSeq}. Computational comparison confirmed that its CDS and protein are identical to historical NM\_007162.2, the accession listed for Addgene \#38119. The older transcript has ten additional terminal adenines outside the CDS. The independently retrieved NP\_009093.1 protein matches the translated CDS.

The empty-vector sequence is fixed to GenBank U55762.1, a 4,733-bp circular pEGFP-N1 reference submitted by Clontech \cite{pEGFPN1Reference}. The published plasmid is a construct precedent, whereas this assignment retains PCR and cloning into the empty backbone. We have not obtained or verified the complete sequence of Addgene \#38119 or a physical vector stock. Kanamycin is the bacterial selection marker; neomycin/G418 is the mammalian selection marker. Stable mammalian selection is not required for the planned transient-expression assay.

For the design, a synthetic cDNA template clone matching the native NM\_007162.3 CDS, including its stop codon and without codon optimization, is proposed. No material has been purchased or experimentally verified. The fusion insert will retain the TFEB initiation ATG but omit its terminal TGA. The complete EGFP coding region is intended to follow in frame and retain its terminal TAA; its ATG will encode an internal methionine. The initiation context and junction sequence still require explicit design and validation. The 1,428-nt TFEB coding portion is not the final tailed amplicon or complete fusion length.

The remaining materials are high-fidelity DNA polymerase, two compatible restriction enzymes, DNA ligase, competent \textit{Escherichia coli} DH5$\alpha$, and kanamycin-containing selection medium. A final plasmid map will be generated after the junctions and all relevant vector features have been verified.

\subsection{Sequence Retrieval and Primer Design}
The versioned TFEB and empty-vector records, extracted CDS and protein, input hashes, and reference-verification script are archived with the project. HindIII/KpnI is the published cloning pair and will be evaluated first \cite{RoczniakFerguson2012,Addgene38119}; it is not yet a validated pair for the designed junctions. Each cloning primer will be reported in the 5$'$ to 3$'$ direction and annotated by protective bases, restriction site, any frame-adjustment or linker bases, and template-annealing sequence. The selected pair must cut the intended backbone without cutting the TFEB insert internally. Primer sequences, annealing-region melting temperatures, final junctions, and the complete fusion translation remain to be determined.

\subsection{PCR Amplification and Restriction Cloning}
The TFEB insert will be amplified with a high-fidelity polymerase. A no-template control and an appropriate amplification control will accompany the reaction. The intended amplicon will be purified, and both insert and vector will be digested and purified before ligation. An initial insert-to-vector molar ratio of approximately 3:1 is proposed. The ligation will be transformed into competent DH5$\alpha$ cells, followed by selection using the backbone's confirmed antibiotic marker. The reaction composition, thermal program, digestion conditions, and transformation details remain to be specified for the selected reagents.

\subsection{Clone Screening and Sequence Verification}
Candidate colonies will be screened by colony PCR and diagnostic restriction digestion. Plasmid DNA from positive clones will be purified for Sanger sequencing with vector-flanking and, where necessary, internal primers. A construct will be accepted only when the complete TFEB insert, both junctions, fusion reading frame, and terminal stop codon match the intended sequence without unintended substitutions, insertions, or deletions. An expected-size band alone will not establish sequence identity.
