\section{Hypothesis-Driven Research Extension}
\subsection{From Replication to a Discriminating Question}
The localization experiments above reproduce established TFEB biology. The extension asks a different question: \textit{does the effect of the C-terminal S466/S467/S469 cluster on target-gene RNA abundance depend on the stimulus and fusion architecture?} Protein abundance and nuclear exposure are measured as possible mediators. An architecture effect is a hypothesis to test, not an explanation already established by the literature.

Paquette et al. reported that the S466A/S467A/S469A mutant (3A) could accumulate in nuclei yet showed impaired transcriptional activity in their experimental systems; they also examined combinations with S142A/S211A \cite{Paquette2021}. Negoita et al. subsequently observed AMPK-associated dephosphorylation of this cluster and explicitly raised the possibility that alanine substitutions affect local structure as well as phosphorylation \cite{Negoita2026}. Their study also distinguished AMPK-dependent responses to MK-8722 from responses to Torin1. Different cell systems, stimuli and constructs prevent treating these findings as a single matched comparison. Neither 3A nor the five-site alanine combination is a new mutant invented here.

Our proposed contribution is a controlled genotype--architecture--stimulus comparison in HeLa cells, followed by selected mechanistic tests. Negoita et al. also obtained supporting phosphosite evidence from C-terminal GFP knock-in material \cite{Negoita2026}; the literature disagreement cannot simply be assigned to GFP. The 3T substitution retains hydroxyl-bearing side chains but remains potentially phosphorylatable; it is a sequence/chemistry probe, not a constitutively dephosphorylated mimic. A targeted literature search supports this rationale but does not establish that the exact combination has never been tested. No claim of first discovery is made.

The two architectures differ in linker, EGFP coding sequence, protein length and the position of the stop junction. Thus ``tag'' in the matrix is shorthand for the entire GDPPVAT--EGFP fusion architecture. A difference cannot identify EGFP itself as the causal component. Attribution to EGFP alone would require sequence-accepted linker-only and appropriately matched fusion controls in a subsequent design; these are not among the five finalized constructs. Drug-associated changes in transgene RNA or protein stability must also be checked before proposing a protein-level tag mechanism.

\subsection{Core Factorial Comparison and Controls}
Cross WT/3A/3T with EGFP-tagged/untagged TFEB and vehicle/Torin1/MK-8722, yielding 18 core conditions (Figure~\ref{fig:research-design}). Perform the principal comparison after endogenous TFEB depletion with one validated 3$'$-UTR siRNA absent from the coding-only constructs. Repeat the complete prespecified WT/3A comparison (both architectures and all three treatments), not just its largest effect, with a second individual TFEB siRNA in separate culture-day blocks. Verify transcript coverage across expressed TFEB isoforms and maintained transgene expression during RNAi. Quantify endogenous depletion by 3$'$-UTR RT-qPCR and protein in matched no-rescue wells. Untagged rescue and residual endogenous TFEB cannot be separated by size; no-rescue wells bound this contamination but do not measure its exact amount in each rescued cell.

Require acceptable viability, at least 80\% endogenous protein depletion in no-rescue wells and pretreatment median TFEB signal per expressing cell within 0.5--2 times the parental reference. These numerical gates are project design choices, not published assay-performance measurements. Untagged WT must restore at least half of the pilot-estimated loss of drug-induced MCOLN1 response, in the same direction, in each drug context for which a TFEB-dependent interpretation is sought. The loss must exceed the pilot assay noise. If this cannot be demonstrated, the drug comparison remains descriptive until the assay is repaired. A defective tagged WT is itself a possible architecture result and is not discarded because it fails untagged-WT rescue performance. Acceptance gates are frozen before confirmatory genotype outcomes are unblinded.

The following controls are run at vehicle, Torin1 and MK-8722 in each independent block. A machine-readable condition manifest accompanies the proposal; counts include culture conditions, not qPCR technical reactions.

NT + WT/3A arms test whether a genotype effect depends on the depletion background; no-rescue arms test loss and baseline drug responsiveness. EGFP-only does not substitute for a nonexpressing control. Add an untreated/no-solvent comparison in the pilot. Keep DNA carrier identity, total DNA mass and total siRNA concentration constant within the DNA-transfected comparisons; reagent-only mocks intentionally lack nucleic acid. DNA molarity is a starting calibration, followed by expression titration rather than a simultaneous claim of equal dose and equal expression. Randomize positions and processing order, balance every contrast across plates, and include shared reference samples if a complete day block spans plates. Record plasmid lot, endotoxin/purity QC and clone identity; do not confound one genotype with one preparation batch. Confirm key effects with the second sequence-accepted clone.

\Needspace{3\baselineskip}
Pilot DNA amounts separately to overlap TFEB abundance and the fraction of expressing cells across constructs, using matched total DNA and transfection reagent. Quantify both architectures with the same anti-TFEB reagent recognizing an epitope outside the changed C-terminal region. Validate specificity by depletion and linearity with antigen-loading series for both architectures. Freeze each construct-specific DNA dose before drug exposure and use that dose in all three treatment arms; do not retitrate after treatment to erase a biological abundance response. In the pretreatment expression pilot, target a between-architecture ratio of 0.8--1.25 for the median TFEB signal among expressing cells and an expressing-fraction difference no greater than 10 percentage points. These are prespecified practical matching tolerances; report the measured differences and uncertainty, not a claim of equivalence based on nonsignificance. Freeze a common threshold above depleted/no-rescue background, use randomly selected fields, and report all-cell distributions, stratified summaries and exclusions. Do not use GFP brightness to select one architecture. If common expression support is absent, revise delivery before the confirmatory comparison. At baseline and 6 h, assay transgene RNA with validated backbone--TFEB-junction and shared TFEB-CDS assays, endogenous RNA with the 3$'$-UTR assay, and protein separately; tag-dependent RNA abundance is an alternative to altered protein activity.

For the extension, use a common nutrient-replete medium and matched final solvent. Pilot MK-8722 at 1, 3 and 10~$\mu$M; retain the Torin1 125/250/500-nM pilot range. Select the lowest tolerated dose with reproducible intended engagement. These are proposed HeLa starting points, not validated operating conditions. Measure p-ACC/total ACC and p-S6K/total S6K under both drugs, plus p-TFEB(S211), TFEB abundance, cell counts and viability. Recheck engagement and tolerability at 6 h; an acute pilot does not qualify a longer exposure. Do not force the two drugs to have equal downstream effects or describe their comparison as a selective AMPK-versus-mTOR mechanism. Report their dose/kinetic differences and retain a second acceptable dose as a sensitivity experiment.

Monitor localization at 0, 30, 60, 120, 240 and 360 min in matched destructive samples, and collect the primary RNA endpoint at 6 h. Retain N/C, nuclear concentration, integrated nuclear TFEB signal and cell/nuclear size. N/C alone can be equal when nuclear amounts differ. Summarize the sampled nuclear-signal trajectory and trapezoidal area as a population exposure proxy; it is not a continuous single-cell trajectory or an absolute molecule count. An intrinsic transactivation interpretation requires overlapping nuclear-signal distributions and comparable trajectories, supported by orthogonal fractionation. Prespecify matching limits from assay precision before confirmation. Differences in exposure remain a potential biological mediator, not a reason to delete the affected samples or automatically adjust the primary effect away.

\subsection{Primary Estimand, Replication and RNA Interpretation}
The primary endpoint is normalized steady-state MCOLN1 RNA abundance at 6 h; LAMP1 and CTSD are secondary. Validate RNA integrity, genomic-DNA removal, primer efficiency, quantification limits and at least two reference genes across all treatments, architectures and depletion backgrounds, following qPCR reporting principles \cite{Bustin2009}. Include no-template/no-RT controls and an inter-run calibrator. A missing amplification is not zero expression; values outside the validated quantitative range are flagged, with a prespecified repeat/exclusion rule. Do not replace an unresponsive primary gene with whichever secondary gene is significant.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
Let $Y_{g,a,s,d}$ denote the mean log$_2$ normalized RNA value from culture wells on independent day $d$, for genotype $g$, architecture $a$ (E, EGFP fusion; U, untagged) and stimulus $s$ (V, vehicle; M, MK-8722; T, Torin1). Average qPCR technical replicates within each sample first. Define
\begin{align}
\Delta_{a,s,d}&=(Y_{3A,a,s,d}-Y_{3A,a,V,d})-(Y_{WT,a,s,d}-Y_{WT,a,V,d}),\\
C_d&=(\Delta_{E,M,d}-\Delta_{U,M,d})-(\Delta_{E,T,d}-\Delta_{U,T,d}).
\end{align}
The primary estimand is the mean $C_d$, the difference between the architecture-dependent 3A effects under the two drugs. Vehicle terms cancel algebraically from $C_d$, but remain essential to estimate each drug response and baseline genotype effects. Report those components even if the interaction is null: equal nonzero effects under both drugs can yield $C_d=0$. The primary result is a total effect in the calibrated expression system. Post-treatment nuclear exposure and protein abundance are possible mediators and are not automatically entered as nuisance covariates; matched-exposure analyses are secondary and cannot alone establish a direct effect.

Use complete independent-day blocks with separate cell culture, RNAi and transfection preparations; distribute technical wells across plate positions. Report day-level $C_d$ values, their mean and a two-sided 95\% interval based on between-day variation (a $t$ interval with $n-1$ degrees of freedom when its assumptions are defensible). Cells, wells, clones and PCR reactions do not inflate $n$. A failed well is not replaced by a cell-level replicate. Prespecify repeat rules from technical QC; if a required arm is unavailable, exclude that incomplete day from the paired primary estimate, retain the available data descriptively, record the reason and acquire a replacement complete block. Outcome size or direction is never an exclusion criterion.

Use a separate pilot of at least four complete day blocks to estimate the variance of $C_d$, then fix the confirmatory sample size before collecting confirmation data. Plan at least six independent days and increase that number as needed for a target 95\% interval half-width of 0.5 log$_2$ units, using a conservative variance estimate from the pilot. This precision target is a design choice, not an observed effect or an equivalence margin; retain the calculation and do not stop when significance is reached. If resources cannot meet the target, label the extension exploratory. Keep pilot and confirmatory data separate. The secondary family comprises the four MCOLN1 $\Delta_{a,s}$ contrasts and the primary-form $C$ contrasts for LAMP1 and CTSD (six tests, Holm adjustment). 3T and non-targeting-background comparisons are explicitly exploratory. The second-siRNA confirmation re-estimates the same primary contrast with its own complete blocks and interval; do not pool the two siRNAs as independent replicates or select the favorable reagent.

\textbf{Synthesis versus stability.} Mature RNA integrates production and degradation \cite{Rabani2011}. Before calling a difference a transcriptional defect, repeat the prespecified WT/3A contrasts with a short, pilot-qualified 4-thiouridine pulse ending at 6 h and quantify newly synthesized MCOLN1 and a secondary target. Include matched unlabeled/background-purification controls, input RNA, labeled/unlabeled recovery standards, cell-count normalization, and checks for labeling toxicity and treatment-dependent incorporation. A recovery spike does not control cellular nucleoside uptake. Freeze pulse duration and performance criteria before confirmation; if incorporation differs, the assay cannot establish differential synthesis. Concordant nascent and mature RNA strengthens a production interpretation; discordance triggers RNA-stability/processing studies. Intronic RT-qPCR can be an exploratory precursor-RNA assay, but is not a definitive substitute. Without a qualified nascent-RNA assay, retain the conclusion ``target-gene RNA abundance'' rather than ``transcription rate.''

\textbf{MiT/TFE compensation.} TFE3 can induce MCOLN1 and related targets even when TFEB is depleted \cite{Martina2014}. Measure TFE3 RNA, protein and localization in the prespecified WT/3A comparison and no-rescue controls, not only after an inconvenient result. If TFE3 activity or residual induction changes, test the key contrasts with TFEB-only, TFE3-only and combined depletion, matched total siRNA, two individual TFE3 reagents and WT-TFEB rescue. Recheck viability and rescue expression in each background. Persistence under competent combined depletion limits a TFEB/TFE3-specific interpretation; loss under TFE3 depletion indicates family-context dependence, not necessarily direct TFEB--TFE3 binding. MITF or other routes remain alternatives. No TFEB-exclusive claim is made from single-factor rescue alone.

\subsection{Mechanistic Follow-Up with Defined Decision Rules}
\textbf{AMPK dependence.} Prespecify WT/3A, both architectures and all three treatments for R03; choosing only the largest R02 difference would be exploratory. Co-deplete PRKAA1 and PRKAA2 using two independent siRNA pairs in separate experiments. Include non-targeting, each single-target depletion, no-rescue and mock controls; match the total siRNA burden, including the concurrent TFEB siRNA. Verify each catalytic subunit, MK-8722-induced p-ACC, p-S6K, viability, nuclear exposure and rescue abundance at the relevant times. A selective loss of the MK-8722 response with a retained Torin1 response supports stimulus-dependent AMPK involvement; Torin1 is a comparator, not a guaranteed AMPK-independent positive control. If both responses fall, evaluate loss of cellular competence and altered baseline expression rather than selecting one literature model. A strong target-specific causal claim additionally requires a near-endogenous, RNAi-resistant AMPK rescue with a matched empty-vector arm; the required isoform/complex and rescue constructs are not sequence-finalized here. Without it, report convergent RNAi evidence for involvement, not definitive on-target mediation. None of these tests identifies a direct TFEB kinase or phosphatase.

\textbf{Phosphosite specificity.} In WT tagged and untagged samples, measure C-terminal phosphorylation together with total TFEB at baseline and after treatment. Phospho-antibodies require phosphatase-treated material, unmodified/phosphorylated peptide controls and site-mutant controls; adjacent residues can affect epitope recognition. Loss of signal in 3A alone is not evidence of physiological dephosphorylation. If specificity is unresolved, use targeted LC--MS/MS with site-localization evidence, appropriate peptide standards and total-TFEB normalization; report ambiguous adjacent-site assignments as such. Enrich both architectures through a validated common TFEB epitope outside the altered region, document input/recovery and include IgG or bead controls. GFP-only capture would systematically exclude the untagged arm. Relative phosphopeptide abundance is not absolute site occupancy without suitable calibration. Analyze 3T with an assay capable of detecting threonine phosphorylation; a serine-phospho-antibody cannot establish its state. This follow-up is conditional on instrument access and assay development.

\textbf{Promoter engagement.} Enter R05 only after a qualified RNA-production difference and comparable nuclear exposure are established. Test TFEB occupancy near validated MCOLN1 and LAMP1 regulatory regions by ChIP--qPCR at matched collection times. Include input, matched IgG, TFEB-depleted/no-rescue material, a negative genomic region and a positive locus with demonstrable WT enrichment. Use a common TFEB antibody outside the changed region; validate equivalent recovery, chromatin fragmentation and crosslinking conditions across architectures. Start from matched cell equivalents and report percent input; percent input alone does not correct for different nuclear TFEB abundance or antibody accessibility. Use a validated spike-in approach if global recovery differs. Reduced occupancy would support altered engagement only after these checks. Comparable occupancy with different nascent RNA would motivate cofactor studies, without proving a particular cofactor mechanism. If these checks fail, report occupancy as unresolved.

\subsection{Additional Workload and Feasibility}
The extension retains five modules after M01--M12: R01, verify the five constructs and qualify expression/depletion; R02, run the factorial comparison, second-siRNA confirmation and conditional RNA-production/family-context checks; R03, test AMPK dependence; R04, resolve phosphosite specificity; and R05, test promoter occupancy only after its entry criteria are met. Weeks 9--12 are a provisional preparation/pilot window, not a promise to finish confirmation and all mechanistic assays. R04--R05 and the conditional R02/R03 additions depend on assay development, resources and preceding results. The eight-week core plan excludes this extension.

At the minimum six confirmatory days and two culture wells per condition, the 18 research conditions alone require 216 wells. Including controls, the primary block requires $45\times6\times2=540$ culture wells, and the second-siRNA WT/3A confirmation requires $39\times6\times2=468$: 1,008 wells \emph{per assay and endpoint} across the two campaigns. These are lower-bound planning counts, not a powered final sample size. They exclude the independent pilot, six-point destructive imaging series, RNA/protein duplicates on separate plates, additional doses, clone checks, R03--R05 and technical PCR reactions. Scaling to multiwell plates requires a density/delivery pilot; the 35-mm-dish doses are not copied unchanged. Release a physical plate map with all controls before execution. If capacity is insufficient, complete R01/R02 first and label the remaining modules deferred; retain all failed and null records. This avoids presenting an unbudgeted matrix as an executable four-week experiment.
