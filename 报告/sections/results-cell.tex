Claim localization only when reproducible independent-day effects agree with protein and imaging quality controls. Claim a TFEB contribution to target-gene RNA responses only after the loss-and-rescue comparison; mature RNA alone does not measure transcription rate. Claim increased operational autophagic turnover only with the paired-inhibitor result. None of these findings alone establishes direct kinase--substrate chemistry, exclusive TFEB mediation, or a universal response outside the tested HeLa conditions.
