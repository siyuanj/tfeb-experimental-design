Localization is concluded when independent-day effects reproduce and agree with the protein and imaging quality controls. A TFEB contribution to target-gene RNA responses is concluded from the loss-and-rescue comparison, and transcription rate from the nascent-RNA assay. Increased operational autophagic turnover is concluded from the paired-inhibitor result. Kinase--substrate chemistry, exclusive TFEB mediation and responses outside the tested HeLa conditions are addressed by the dedicated modules and the second-cell-system confirmation described in the Discussion.
