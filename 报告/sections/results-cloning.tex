\subsection{Molecular Cloning}
PCR is expected to produce an amplicon matching the finalized TFEB insert design, with no corresponding product in the no-template control. Colony PCR and diagnostic digestion should yield the predicted fragment pattern. Exact amplicon and restriction-fragment lengths will be recorded with the completed sequence design.

The decisive criterion is Sanger confirmation of the complete intended fusion construct. Successful cloning requires correct insert identity and orientation, both junctions, the continuous open reading frame, and the intended termination codon. The final report will include a plasmid map, a cloning workflow, and explicitly labeled schematic predictions for the gel patterns.
