Flanking PCR and HindIII/BamHI digestion cannot exclude reversed TFEB; the proposed gel cannot resolve three-base retained-stop differences. Acceptance requires the expected digest and actual, unambiguous, concordant bidirectional sequence across 602--2799 (2,198 nt), including both junctions, the complete fusion ORF and terminal stop. Ten primers predict usable coverage in both directions at every target base, not experimental coverage or whole-backbone verification. Resolve low-quality calls, mixed peaks, gaps and unexpected target changes under the Sanger acceptance procedure before cell assays.
