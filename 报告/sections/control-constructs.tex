\subsection{Sequence-Defined Control and Mutant Constructs}\label{sec:control-constructs}
Five plasmid conditions are defined: parental pEGFP-N1 expressing EGFP only, the accepted WT TFEB--EGFP reporter, and three derived reporters carrying S211A, S142A or S142A/S211A (Figures~\ref{fig:control-plasmids} and \ref{fig:mutant-plasmids}). Amino-acid positions refer to human NP\_009093.1. The fusion constructs share the same 6,134-bp plasmid architecture, the GDPPVAT junction and C-terminal EGFP, and encode 722 residues. The single mutants and their combination are compared with WT under each treatment, which separates site-specific from combined contributions~\cite{Vega2017,Napolitano2018}.

Each mutant is generated from a sequence-defined synthetic donor that carries the same cloning flanks as the WT amplicon, followed by the HindIII/BamHI workflow above. The donor FASTA files are in the design record. The WT cloning primers anneal outside the mutated codons and amplify each donor, so no mutagenesis primers are needed. Each batch includes a no-template PCR, a digested-vector-only ligation and the positive transformation control. Sanger reads cover the full fusion region and both junctions in both directions, with the target codons read explicitly. Two independently recovered, sequence-correct bacterial clones per genotype confirm the key contrasts and detect clone- or preparation-specific effects; clone identity is randomized across experimental days. Bacterial clones serve as technical confirmation, and independent culture days remain the biological replicates.

The derived sequences keep the WT reading frame and the unique HindIII/BamHI cleavage geometry. Their digest and colony-PCR patterns therefore match WT, and full-length bidirectional sequencing identifies each mutant. Each construct passes three gates in order: sequence acceptance, intact full-length expression (anti-GFP and anti-TFEB blots), then phenotype. Alanine removes the serine phospho-acceptor and also alters local interactions; the RNAi and phospho-specificity modules address kinase identity. Phosphomimetic S-to-D/E variants are omitted because they do not reliably reproduce the phosphorylated state.
