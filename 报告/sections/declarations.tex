\section*{AI Use Declaration}
AI assistance (OpenAI Codex) was used for literature checking, sequence-design code, local computational checks, drafting and integration, figure preparation, and LaTeX compilation and layout review. The circular maps were rendered with SnapGene Viewer from sequence-verified display copies; their English size-unit labels and panel layout were typeset from native SVG exports. The NPG/nrc palette follows the open-source ggsci palette inspired by Nature Reviews Cancer. The cloning and evidence-flow illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, corrected after inspection, traced into vector paths with VTracer, and combined with editable labels and arrows. Their visual approach was informed by the Bioicons and D2 GitHub projects; no third-party icon was copied. Aligned fusion cassettes, sampling, the factorial matrix and predicted band positions were drawn by code from declared design choices and accepted sequence/fragment tables. Earlier draft illustrations remain archived. Computational checks and conceptual artwork are distinct from experimental observations. Source images, prompts, calculation inputs and reproducible scripts accompany the design. The authors remain responsible for verifying the scientific claims, practical feasibility, final parameters and contribution statement before submission.
