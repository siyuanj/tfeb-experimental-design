\subsection{Cell Quality, Expression and Treatment}
Use authenticated, mycoplasma-negative HeLa cells from a recorded source and restricted passage range. Distribute the same cell suspension across each experimental block. Culture in DMEM with 10\% FBS at 37\,$^\circ$C and 5\% CO$_2$, aiming for 60--70\% confluence at transfection. Pilot 0.10, 0.25 and 0.50\,$\mu$g fusion plasmid per 35-mm dish with one lipid reagent at its documented DNA ratio; evaluate expression after 24 h. The range follows published HeLa transfection work~\cite{RoczniakFerguson2012}. Select the lowest dose giving adequate nonsaturated signal, retain a second acceptable dose for sensitivity analysis, and compare the selected dose with endogenous TFEB abundance by anti-TFEB blot and IF; the reporter-to-endogenous ratio is reported. Plasmid molarity and total DNA mass are matched across constructs with a verified nonexpressing carrier, because equal mass gives different molecule numbers for the 4,733-bp empty and 6,134-bp fusion vectors.

The primary nutrient comparison uses a defined DMEM-based formulation with or without its amino-acid supplement, including glutamine; glucose, salts, vitamins and 10\% dialyzed serum remain matched. Refeeding restores that amino-acid mixture after 60 min. A separate amino-acid-replete, serum-free arm tests serum withdrawal. The medium formulation and supplier record are frozen before execution. Fed and depleted wells receive matched washes and medium exchanges.

The Torin1 starting condition is 250 nM for 60 min, preceded by a 125/250/500 nM pilot at 30/60 min. Published work establishes Torin1 responsiveness of TFEB~\cite{Vega2017}; the pilot optimizes dose and duration for this transient-expression protocol. DMSO is matched to the highest concentration used, capped at 0.1\%, in relevant controls. The lowest exposure with reproducible pathway engagement and acceptable viability is selected. Viability criteria are at least 90\% membrane-intact cells and no more than a 10-percentage-point loss versus vehicle, including detached cells. Unsuitable conditions are rejected during the pilot, before confirmatory TFEB effects are inspected.

Acute localization is collected at 0/15/30/60 min in separate matched wells. For recovery, collect after 60-min starvation and after 15/30/60-min refeeding, with continuously fed and continued-starvation wells at 75/90/120 min total time. This series applies to WT and all three mutants. RNA, co-IP and flux use separate plates. Figure~\ref{fig:control-schedule} shows acute and recovery sampling.

\subsection{Imaging, Endogenous Confirmation and Sampling}
Start with 4\% paraformaldehyde fixation for 10 min and DAPI counterstaining; GFP preservation is checked in the pilot. Permeabilization and endogenous TFEB antibody dilution are optimized in parental cells. Include no-primary staining and show signal reduction with each TFEB siRNA before quantitative use. Acquisition settings are identical and nonsaturating within each day; plate positions and acquisition order are randomized, images are coded for analysis and raw files are retained. A 60-min leptomycin B arm on WT (starting at 20 nM, titrated in the pilot) defines the achievable nuclear ceiling, and each genotype's N/C is also reported as a fraction of that ceiling.

Nuclei are segmented with DAPI and cells with a compatible boundary marker or validated transmitted-light method, rather than a treatment-dependent GFP threshold. Nuclei and neighboring cells are excluded from cytoplasmic masks. N/C uses background-corrected mean intensities:
\begin{equation}
R_{\mathrm{N/C}}=\frac{I_{\mathrm{nucleus}}-I_{\mathrm{background}}}{I_{\mathrm{cytoplasm}}-I_{\mathrm{background}}}.
\end{equation}
Component intensities are retained. Out-of-focus, saturated, mitotic, dead, overlapping or unsegmentable cells are excluded under pilot-frozen rules. Cytoplasmic intensity at or below background plus three background standard deviations is nonquantifiable and its frequency is reported in place of an arbitrary large ratio. Exclusions are reported by group to expose treatment-dependent selection.

The plan is four independent culture/transfection days after a separate pilot, two technical wells per condition, and at least 30 eligible cells per well from at least five prespecified nonoverlapping fields. All eligible cells in those fields are analyzed, or a random subsample is drawn with a recorded seed. Cells, fields and technical wells are nested observations, and the independent culture day is the biological replicate. Key WT treatment/recovery contrasts are confirmed in parental cells by endogenous TFEB IF and fractionation; agreement of direction and size supports endogenous consistency.

\subsection{Protein Expression, Phosphorylation and Fractionation}
Anti-GFP and anti-TFEB whole-cell blots check intact expression. The WT fusion has \FusionAa{} residues and a theoretical unmodified mass of \FusionMassKDa{} kDa; mutants have the same length and a slightly different exact mass. A low band is assigned as cleavage when anti-GFP and anti-TFEB blots identify complementary fragments. Linear detection is established with a pilot loading series, then loading starts at 15--20\,$\mu$g protein per lane. Phosphorylation is preserved with appropriate inhibitors, uncropped membranes and markers are shown, and abundance is normalized to total-protein staining. A housekeeping band is used after its stability is checked.

p-TFEB(S211)/total TFEB is probed at the fusion-sized band and p-S6K(T389)/total S6K in the same lysates. S211A and a separate phosphatase-treated aliquot test phospho-antibody specificity; the phosphatase aliquot uses compatible buffer without inhibitor, and the untreated aliquot receives matched handling. The phospho/total ratio uses the fusion-sized band alone. p-S6K reports pathway engagement and p-TFEB(S211) reports the site.

Nuclear/cytoplasmic fractionation compares matched cell equivalents and corrects for fraction volumes and recovery. Lamin B1 or histone H3 marks nuclei; tubulin marks cytoplasm. Reciprocal contamination is assessed and whole-cell input is retained. Redistribution is concluded from volume-corrected fractions with clean purity markers that agree with endogenous IF.

\subsection{Site Perturbations, Genetic Attribution and Binding}
The sequence-defined panel is described in Section~\ref{sec:control-constructs}. S211A tests loss of that phospho-acceptor; S142A and the double mutant test additional recovery-associated regulation. S211A raises basal nuclear localization, while further mTORC1 sites such as S122 keep TFEB under mTORC1 control~\cite{Vega2017}. Refeeding-related nuclear clearance measures net redistribution. S142/S138 have been linked to nuclear export~\cite{Napolitano2018}; fixed-cell N/C curves report net redistribution, and import and export rates are separated by live-cell cytoplasmic FRAP with unbleached/photobleaching controls or an export-inhibitor comparison.

For mTOR-complex attribution, two nonoverlapping siRNAs per target are tested individually against RPTOR or RICTOR, with equal-concentration non-targeting siRNA and reagent-only mock. The pilot starts at 10 nM for 72 h, with reporter transfection during the last 24 h. Depletion is verified in each experimental block, and p-S6K/total S6K and p-AKT(S473)/total AKT are assayed. Each depletion is compared with non-targeting control under fed conditions, with Torin1 as a response reference. A RICTOR result is interpreted when depletion is verified and a measurable mTORC2 readout is present; a matched stimulated positive-control lysate is included if basal p-AKT is too low. The genetic comparison is literature-supported~\cite{Martina2012}. RPTOR RNAi provides the mTORC1-specific test, since Torin1 inhibits both mTOR complexes. An RNAi-resistant RPTOR rescue construct, sequence-defined at procurement, gives the target-specific confirmation.

For co-IP, WT and S211A are compared under vehicle/Torin1 using GFP-affinity capture from equal total-protein inputs. EGFP-only, beads-only and input fractions are included; antibody-based IP adds a matched isotype control. 14-3-3 and recovered fusion are detected, and co-precipitated 14-3-3 is normalized to recovered TFEB--EGFP. Reduced association at equal bait recovery supports phosphorylation-dependent complex formation; bead-only binding and bait recovery are scored first.

\subsection{TFEB Depletion, Rescue and Target-Gene Expression}
Two independent siRNAs against the TFEB 3$'$ UTR are tested individually, so the coding-only B-v1 transgene lacks the target sites. Before ordering, targets are verified against NM\_007162.3 and expressed isoforms, off-target complementarity is checked and exact sequences are recorded. RNAi resistance requires both absence of the target site and maintained rescue expression during RNAi. siRNA sequences are verified at procurement.

For each of fed vehicle and sustained Torin1, the arms are: non-targeting siRNA + empty vector; each TFEB siRNA + empty vector; each TFEB siRNA + WT; and non-targeting siRNA + WT. Reagent-only controls are included in the pilot. Plasmid molarity and total DNA are matched; endogenous depletion is quantified separately from fusion expression, and rescue is titrated close to endogenous abundance. The control-siRNA + WT arm detects effects of rescue overexpression. RNAi runs 72 h with WT rescue during the final 24 h. Mutant rescue follows successful WT rescue.

\textit{MCOLN1}, \textit{LAMP1} and \textit{CTSD} transcripts are measured at 4/8 h on separate plates with matched time controls. The panel and timing test the output of the established TFEB transcriptional program~\cite{Settembre2011}. \textit{RPLP0}, \textit{HPRT1} and \textit{TBP} are piloted, and at least two stable reference genes are retained. New qPCR primers are validated for specificity, a single product, 90--110\% efficiency and quantitative range, with clean no-template/no-RT controls, documented RNA quality and an inter-run calibrator \cite{Bustin2009}. Exact oligos are fixed at procurement. Normalized Cq differences are analyzed, with $2^{-\Delta\Delta Cq}$ used when efficiency assumptions hold. Biological $n$ is the independent culture day; technical PCR wells are averaged. These mature-RNA measurements integrate synthesis and decay; the nascent-RNA controls of the research extension address transcription rate.

The pilot also compares TFEB-only with combined TFEB + TFE3 depletion for residual drug-induced MCOLN1. If TFEB-only depletion retains at least half of the induction (pilot estimate), the combined background is adopted and frozen before confirmation. A response reduced by both TFEB siRNAs and restored by WT supports a TFEB contribution. Residual induction is attributed to incomplete depletion, TFE3/MITF compensation or parallel regulation, and TFE3 abundance/localization are assayed when substantial residual induction persists. A failed rescue is interpreted after rescue abundance, localization and RNAi resistance are confirmed.

\subsection{Autophagic Turnover and Toxicity}
On separate plates, LC3-I/LC3-II and SQSTM1/p62 are measured with and without a terminal 60-min pulse of 100 nM bafilomycin A1 during a 4-h Torin1 exposure, following consensus autophagy-flux practice~\cite{Klionsky2021}. A first pilot tests 50/100 nM for 30/60 min for blockade and tolerability. Each stimulus and knockdown/rescue condition has its own inhibitor-free and inhibitor-treated well: vehicle, bafilomycin alone, stimulus alone and combination. Operational turnover is the within-condition inhibitor-dependent increment of normalized LC3-II, compared across stimulus and TFEB loss/rescue. This paired-inhibitor logic follows experimental TFEB/autophagy work~\cite{Settembre2011}.

p62 transcript is measured alongside protein, since transcription changes alter protein abundance. Viability and loading are checked in every arm. More LC3-II without additional inhibitor-dependent accumulation points to impaired clearance. mTOR inhibition also acts through TFEB-independent pathways, so TFEB depletion may leave part of the response intact. Localization is scored in bafilomycin-free wells. If turnover remains ambiguous, a tandem mCherry--GFP--LC3 assay with inhibitor and single-channel controls provides an orthogonal readout (reporter plasmid selected at procurement).

\subsection{Prespecified Analysis and Decision Rules}
Primary contrasts are WT amino-acid starvation vs replete medium at 60 min; WT Torin1 vs vehicle at 60 min; and WT 30-min recovery vs continued starvation at 90 min total time. Cell log$_2$(N/C) is averaged within well and then across independent day/condition. Paired day-level contrasts are analyzed with effects and 95\% confidence intervals, and Holm correction applies across the three primary tests. Four independent days is the minimum; pilot variance and the effect threshold below set the confirmatory sample size before that dataset is collected.

\textbf{Success criteria for the replication.} The reporter is accepted as responsive when each primary contrast has a Holm-adjusted $p<0.05$, a mean day-level change in log$_2$(N/C) of at least $\log_2 1.5$ in the expected direction (increase for starvation and Torin1, decrease for recovery), and the same sign on every independent day. The pilot may revise the $\log_2 1.5$ threshold, and any revision is recorded before confirmation. Reporter acceptance also requires matched direction in endogenous TFEB IF and clean fractionation.

For mutants, a genotype-by-condition/time model uses day as a block and prespecified WT-versus-mutant interaction contrasts. Recovery analysis accounts for each genotype's starvation-endpoint ratio, since different initial ratios do not by themselves indicate different recovery rates. If sample size or model assumptions fail, day-level contrasts with uncertainty are reported and the planned extra replicates are collected. RNAi/rescue, transcripts, co-IP and turnover are separate secondary families with multiplicity control. Plots show independent-day summaries with nested cell distributions. Equivalence claims use a prespecified margin. Discordant assays trigger the relevant control checks.
