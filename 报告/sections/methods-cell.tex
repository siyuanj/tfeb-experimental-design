\subsection{Cell Quality, Expression and Treatment}
Use authenticated, mycoplasma-negative HeLa cells from a recorded source and restricted passage range. Distribute the same cell suspension across each experimental block. Culture in DMEM with 10\% FBS at 37\,$^\circ$C and 5\% CO$_2$, aiming for 60--70\% confluence at transfection. Pilot 0.10, 0.25 and 0.50\,$\mu$g fusion plasmid per 35-mm dish with one lipid reagent at its documented DNA ratio; evaluate expression after 24 h. The range is a project choice informed by published HeLa transfection work~\cite{RoczniakFerguson2012}. Select the lowest dose giving adequate nonsaturated signal and retain a second acceptable dose for sensitivity analysis. Match plasmid molarity across constructs and total DNA mass using a verified nonexpressing carrier. Equal mass alone does not match molecule number for the 4,733-bp empty and 6,134-bp fusion vectors.

The primary nutrient comparison uses a defined DMEM-based formulation with or without its amino-acid supplement, including glutamine; glucose, salts, vitamins and 10\% dialyzed serum remain matched. Refeeding restores that amino-acid mixture after 60 min. A separate amino-acid-replete, serum-free arm tests serum withdrawal. Freeze the medium formulation and supplier record before execution. Fed and depleted wells receive matched washes and medium exchanges.

The proposed Torin1 starting condition is 250 nM for 60 min, preceded by a 125/250/500 nM pilot at 30/60 min. Literature establishes responsiveness, not optimization of this transient-expression protocol~\cite{Vega2017}. Match DMSO to the highest concentration used, capped at 0.1\%, in relevant controls. Select the lowest exposure with reproducible pathway engagement and acceptable viability. Prespecify at least 90\% membrane-intact cells and no more than a 10-percentage-point loss versus vehicle, including detached cells. Reject unsuitable conditions during the pilot, before inspecting confirmatory TFEB effects.

Collect acute localization at 0/15/30/60 min in separate matched wells. For recovery, collect after 60-min starvation and after 15/30/60-min refeeding; include continuously fed and continued-starvation wells at 75/90/120 min total time. Apply this series to WT and all three mutants. RNA, co-IP and flux use separate plates. Figure~\ref{fig:control-schedule} shows acute and recovery sampling.

\subsection{Imaging, Endogenous Confirmation and Sampling}
Start with 4\% paraformaldehyde fixation for 10 min and DAPI counterstaining; check GFP preservation in the pilot. Optimize permeabilization and endogenous TFEB antibody dilution in parental cells. Include no-primary staining and show signal reduction with each TFEB siRNA before quantitative use. Keep acquisition settings identical and nonsaturating within each day; randomize plate positions and acquisition order, code images for analysis and retain raw files.

Segment nuclei with DAPI and cells with a compatible boundary marker or validated transmitted-light method, rather than a treatment-dependent GFP threshold. Exclude nuclei and neighboring cells from cytoplasmic masks. Use background-corrected mean intensities:
\begin{equation}
R_{\mathrm{N/C}}=\frac{I_{\mathrm{nucleus}}-I_{\mathrm{background}}}{I_{\mathrm{cytoplasm}}-I_{\mathrm{background}}}.
\end{equation}
Retain component intensities. Exclude out-of-focus, saturated, mitotic, dead, overlapping or unsegmentable cells under pilot-frozen rules. Cytoplasmic intensity at or below background plus three background standard deviations is nonquantifiable; report its frequency rather than assigning an arbitrary large ratio. Report exclusions by group to detect treatment-dependent selection.

Plan four independent culture/transfection days after a separate pilot, two technical wells per condition, and at least 30 eligible cells per well from at least five prespecified nonoverlapping fields. Analyze all eligible cells in those fields or randomly subsample with a recorded seed. Cells, fields and technical wells are nested observations, not independent biological replicates. Confirm key WT treatment/recovery contrasts in parental cells by endogenous TFEB IF and fractionation. Agreement supports endogenous consistency, not equivalence between tagged and native protein.

\subsection{Protein Expression, Phosphorylation and Fractionation}
Use anti-GFP and anti-TFEB whole-cell blots to check intact expression. The WT fusion has \FusionAa{} residues and a theoretical unmodified mass of \FusionMassKDa{} kDa; mutants retain its length but not its exact mass. A low band is not assigned as cleavage solely by size. Establish linear detection with a pilot loading series, then start with 15--20\,$\mu$g protein per lane. Preserve phosphorylation with appropriate inhibitors, show uncropped membranes and markers, and normalize abundance to total-protein staining. Use a housekeeping band only after checking stability.

Probe p-TFEB(S211)/total TFEB at the fusion-sized band and p-S6K(T389)/total S6K. S211A and a separate phosphatase-treated aliquot test phospho-antibody specificity. Use compatible phosphatase buffer without inhibitor for that aliquot and matched handling for the untreated aliquot. Do not mix endogenous and fusion bands in the phospho/total ratio. p-S6K is pathway engagement, not a substitute for p-TFEB.

For nuclear/cytoplasmic fractionation, compare matched cell equivalents and correct for fraction volumes/recovery. Lamin B1 or histone H3 marks nuclei; tubulin marks cytoplasm. Assess reciprocal contamination and retain whole-cell input. Independently equal-mass-loaded fractions alone cannot establish redistribution. Require clean fractions and agreement with endogenous IF for a supported endogenous-localization claim.

\subsection{Site Perturbations, Genetic Attribution and Binding}
The sequence-defined panel is described in Section~\ref{sec:control-constructs}. S211A tests loss of that phospho-acceptor; S142A and the double mutant test additional recovery-associated regulation. S211A can retain Torin1 responsiveness~\cite{Vega2017}. Refeeding-related nuclear clearance measures net redistribution. S142/S138 have been linked to nuclear export~\cite{Napolitano2018}, but fixed-cell N/C curves cannot separate import and export rates. A specific export-kinetics claim would require live-cell cytoplasmic FRAP with unbleached/photobleaching controls, or an export-inhibitor comparison.

For mTOR-complex attribution, test two nonoverlapping siRNAs per target individually against RPTOR or RICTOR, plus equal-concentration non-targeting siRNA and reagent-only mock. Start a pilot at 10 nM for 72 h, with reporter transfection during the last 24 h. Verify depletion in each experimental block and assay p-S6K/total S6K and p-AKT(S473)/total AKT. Compare each depletion with non-targeting control under fed conditions; retain Torin1 as a response reference. A negative RICTOR result is informative only with verified depletion and a measurable mTORC2 readout; include a matched stimulated positive-control lysate if basal p-AKT is too low. This genetic comparison is literature-supported~\cite{Martina2012}; Torin1 alone cannot establish mTORC1 specificity. RNAi-resistant RPTOR rescue would be required for a stronger target-specific RNAi causal claim; that additional construct is not sequence-finalized here.

For co-IP, compare WT/S211A under vehicle/Torin1 using GFP-affinity capture from equal total-protein inputs. Include EGFP-only, beads-only and input fractions; if antibody-based IP is used, include a matched isotype control. Detect 14-3-3 and recovered fusion; normalize co-precipitated 14-3-3 to recovered TFEB--EGFP. Reduced association with equal bait recovery supports phosphorylation-associated complex formation. Nonspecific bead binding or unequal bait recovery prevents that inference. Co-IP alone does not establish direct contact.

\subsection{TFEB Depletion, Rescue and Transcription}
Select two independent siRNAs against the TFEB 3$'$ UTR, tested individually, so the coding-only B-v1 transgene lacks the intended sites. Before ordering, verify targets against NM\_007162.3 and expressed isoforms, check off-target complementarity and record exact sequences. Resistance requires both absence of the target site and maintained rescue expression during RNAi; it is not claimed from the label ``3$'$-UTR siRNA.'' No new siRNA is presented as sequence-validated here.

For each of fed vehicle and sustained Torin1 include: non-targeting siRNA + empty vector; each TFEB siRNA + empty vector; each TFEB siRNA + WT; and non-targeting siRNA + WT. Include reagent-only controls in the pilot. Match plasmid molarity/total DNA; quantify endogenous depletion separately from fusion expression and titrate rescue close to endogenous abundance. The control-siRNA + WT arm detects effects of rescue overexpression. Start with 72-h RNAi and WT rescue during the final 24 h. Mutant rescue is secondary to successful WT rescue.

Measure \textit{MCOLN1}, \textit{LAMP1} and \textit{CTSD} transcripts at 4/8 h on separate plates with matched time controls. The panel and timing are project choices testing output of the established TFEB transcriptional program~\cite{Settembre2011}. Pilot \textit{RPLP0}, \textit{HPRT1} and \textit{TBP}, retaining at least two stable reference genes. Validate new qPCR primers for specificity, a single product, 90--110\% efficiency and clean no-template/no-RT controls. Exact oligos remain a procurement-stage requirement. Analyze normalized Cq differences; use $2^{-\Delta\Delta Cq}$ only when efficiency assumptions hold. Technical PCR wells do not increase biological $n$.

A response reduced by both TFEB siRNAs and restored by WT supports a TFEB contribution. Residual induction may reflect incomplete depletion, TFE3/MITF compensation or parallel regulation, rather than prove TFEB independence. If substantial residual induction persists, assay TFE3 abundance/localization before claiming exclusivity. Failed rescue is interpretable only after confirming rescue abundance, localization and RNAi resistance.

\subsection{Autophagic Turnover and Toxicity}
On separate plates measure LC3-I/LC3-II and SQSTM1/p62 with/without a terminal 60-min pulse of 100 nM bafilomycin A1 during a proposed 4-h Torin1 exposure. First pilot 50/100 nM for 30/60 min for blockade and tolerability; these doses are not established optima here. Each stimulus and knockdown/rescue condition needs its own inhibitor-free and inhibitor-treated well: vehicle, bafilomycin alone, stimulus alone and combination. Define operational turnover as the within-condition inhibitor-dependent increment of normalized LC3-II. Compare increments across stimulus and TFEB loss/rescue, not raw band size. This paired-inhibitor logic follows experimental TFEB/autophagy work~\cite{Settembre2011}.

Measure p62 transcript alongside protein because transcription changes can confound protein abundance. Check viability and loading in every arm. More LC3-II without additional inhibitor-dependent accumulation is compatible with impaired clearance. mTOR inhibition also acts through TFEB-independent pathways; TFEB depletion need not abolish the entire response. Do not use bafilomycin-treated wells to infer unperturbed TFEB localization. If turnover remains ambiguous, add a separate tandem mCherry--GFP--LC3 assay with inhibitor and single-channel controls; this is a conditional orthogonal assay, not a sequence-designed plasmid in this panel.

\subsection{Prespecified Analysis and Decision Rules}
Primary contrasts are WT amino-acid starvation vs replete medium at 60 min; WT Torin1 vs vehicle at 60 min; and WT 30-min recovery vs continued starvation at 90 min total time. Average cell log$_2$(N/C) within well and then independent day/condition. Analyze paired day-level contrasts, report effects and 95\% confidence intervals, and apply Holm correction across the three primary tests. Four days is a minimum replication plan, not a power calculation: use separate pilot variance and a stated meaningful effect to determine confirmatory sample size before collecting that dataset.

For mutants use a genotype-by-condition/time model with day as a block and prespecified WT-versus-mutant interaction contrasts. Account for each genotype's starvation-endpoint ratio in recovery analysis; different initial ratios are not automatically different recovery rates. If sample size/model assumptions fail, report day-level contrasts with uncertainty and collect the planned extra replicates. RNAi/rescue, transcripts, co-IP and turnover are separate secondary families with multiplicity control. Plot independent-day summaries with nested cell distributions. A nonsignificant result is not equivalence. Discordant assays remain visible and trigger the relevant control checks; no effect sizes, error bars or significance are invented for this proposal.
