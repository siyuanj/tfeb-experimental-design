\begin{abstract}
Transcription factor EB (TFEB) connects nutrient signaling to lysosomal and autophagy-related gene expression. This proposal combines controlled replication of TFEB redistribution with a hypothesis-driven investigation of C-terminal regulation in HeLa cells. The accepted WT reporter is \ConstructBp{} bp and encodes \FusionAa{} residues, with a theoretical unmodified mass of \FusionMassKDa{} kDa. EGFP-only, S211A, S142A and S142A/S211A controls support matched starvation, Torin1 and refeeding experiments. Endogenous measurements, expression titration, pathway perturbation and TFEB loss/rescue address alternative explanations. A research extension compares WT, S466A/S467A/S469A (3A) and S466T/S467T/S469T (3T) with or without the linker--EGFP fusion under vehicle, Torin1 or MK-8722. Five additional sequence-defined constructs test architecture- and stimulus-dependent effects on target-gene RNA. Nuclear exposure, RNA production versus stability, and TFE3 compensation constrain mechanistic interpretation. Phosphosite and promoter-occupancy assays are conditional follow-ups. Independent culture-day replication, coded analysis and a prespecified interaction contrast govern inference. DNA calculations and maps are in-silico outputs; no cellular outcome is presented as measured data.
\keywords{TFEB; EGFP; phosphorylation; experimental controls; nuclear localization; autophagic turnover}
\end{abstract}
