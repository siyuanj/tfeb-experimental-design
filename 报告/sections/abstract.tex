\begin{abstract}
Transcription factor EB (TFEB) connects nutrient signaling to lysosomal and autophagy-related gene expression. This proposal reproduces nutrient- and Torin1-dependent TFEB redistribution in HeLa cells with a newly designed reporter, then tests how the C-terminal S466/S467/S469 cluster controls target-gene RNA. The WT reporter is \ConstructBp{} bp, encodes \FusionAa{} residues and has a theoretical unmodified mass of \FusionMassKDa{} kDa; it is built by HindIII/BamHI directional cloning into pEGFP-N1 and accepted after bidirectional Sanger sequencing. EGFP-only, S211A, S142A and S142A/S211A controls support matched starvation, Torin1 and refeeding experiments, backed by endogenous TFEB measurements, expression titration, RPTOR/RICTOR RNAi and TFEB loss-and-rescue. The research extension compares WT, S466A/S467A/S469A (3A) and S466T/S467T/S469T (3T) with or without the linker--EGFP fusion under vehicle, Torin1 or the AMPK activator MK-8722; five additional sequence-defined constructs complete the six-construct matrix, and two linker-only constructs attribute any architecture effect to the linker or to EGFP. The primary endpoint is MCOLN1 RNA at 6 h, analyzed as the architecture-dependent 3A effect under each drug and its difference between drugs. Nuclear exposure, nascent RNA, TFE3 compensation, AMPK and Akt dependence, phosphosite identity and promoter occupancy are secondary or conditional modules with stated decision rules. Independent culture days are the unit of replication, and the primary estimands are prespecified genotype $\times$ architecture $\times$ stimulus contrasts.
\keywords{TFEB; EGFP; phosphorylation; experimental controls; nuclear localization; autophagic turnover}
\end{abstract}
