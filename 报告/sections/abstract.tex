\begin{abstract}
Transcription factor EB (TFEB) is a nutrient-responsive transcription factor that coordinates autophagy and lysosomal biogenesis. This proposal aims to construct an EGFP-tagged human TFEB expression plasmid and use it to visualize TFEB localization in HeLa cells under nutrient-replete, starvation, and mTOR-inhibition conditions. The central hypothesis is that nutrient deprivation or pharmacological inhibition of mTOR will increase nuclear accumulation of the reporter. The construct will be generated by PCR amplification of a defined human TFEB coding sequence, restriction-enzyme cloning into a mammalian expression vector, bacterial screening, and Sanger sequencing. Transfected cells will be evaluated by fluorescence microscopy and immunoblotting. All outcomes described here are expected results of a proposed experiment.
\keywords{TFEB; autophagy; lysosome; mTORC1; EGFP fusion protein; subcellular localization}
\end{abstract}
