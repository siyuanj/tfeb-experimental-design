\begin{abstract}
Transcription factor EB (TFEB) connects nutrient signaling to lysosomal and autophagy-related gene expression. This proposal combines controlled replication of TFEB redistribution with a hypothesis-driven investigation of C-terminal regulation in HeLa cells. The accepted WT reporter is \ConstructBp{} bp and encodes \FusionAa{} residues, with a theoretical unmodified mass of \FusionMassKDa{} kDa. EGFP-only, S211A, S142A and S142A/S211A controls support matched starvation, Torin1 and refeeding experiments. Endogenous measurements, expression titration, pathway perturbation and TFEB loss/rescue address alternative explanations. A research extension compares WT, S466A/S467A/S469A (3A) and S466T/S467T/S469T (3T) with or without EGFP under vehicle, Torin1 or MK-8722. Five additional sequence-defined constructs test whether transcriptional effects depend on tag and stimulus after accounting for abundance and nuclear exposure. Phosphosite validation and conditional promoter-occupancy assays distinguish competing interpretations. Independent culture-day replication, coded analysis and prespecified contrasts govern inference. DNA calculations and maps are in-silico outputs; no cellular outcome is presented as measured data.
\keywords{TFEB; EGFP; phosphorylation; experimental controls; nuclear localization; autophagic turnover}
\end{abstract}
