\section{Rationale and Research Basis}
TFEB is a member of the MiT/TFE transcription-factor family and a major transcriptional regulator of the autophagy--lysosome pathway. Under nutrient-rich conditions, mTORC1-dependent phosphorylation favors cytoplasmic retention of TFEB. During nutrient starvation or mTOR inhibition, TFEB accumulates in the nucleus and activates genes involved in lysosomal biogenesis and cellular clearance \cite{Settembre2012,RoczniakFerguson2012}. Nutrient-dependent control of nuclear export also contributes to TFEB distribution \cite{Napolitano2018}.

HeLa cells expressing TFEB--GFP are an established system for measuring nuclear accumulation after starvation or Torin1 treatment \cite{RoczniakFerguson2012}. This project uses that response to qualify a newly designed reporter, and the primary readout is the nuclear-to-cytoplasmic (N/C) distribution of the fusion protein. Additional modules measure reversibility, agreement with endogenous TFEB and downstream function. The construct is sequence-verified before any cell experiment.

\subsection{Aims and Hypotheses}
\textbf{Aim 1} constructs and sequence-verifies an EGFP-tagged TFEB mammalian expression plasmid. \textbf{Aim 2} tests nutrient- and drug-dependent redistribution and recovery with reporter and endogenous measurements. \textbf{Aim 3} assesses the contribution of S142, S211 and the mTOR complexes, and the dependence of target-gene RNA and autophagic turnover on TFEB. \textbf{Aim 4} tests whether the effect of the C-terminal S466/S467/S469 cluster depends on the fusion architecture and on the stimulus, and attributes any architecture effect to the linker or to EGFP.

The replication hypothesis is that amino-acid starvation increases the N/C fluorescence ratio relative to time-matched nutrient-replete culture, that Torin1 increases it relative to matched vehicle, and that refeeding after starvation lowers it relative to continued starvation. The Aim 4 hypothesis is that substituting the C-terminal cluster changes target-gene RNA differently with and without the linker--EGFP fusion and differently under Torin1 and MK-8722. Aim 4 follows from unresolved interpretation of this cluster (Section~\ref{sec:extension}); it has its own factorial design and decision rules.
