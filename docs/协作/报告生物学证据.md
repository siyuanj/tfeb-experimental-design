## 对照与 WB 接口：设计判断

以下是基于读出逻辑的**我的设计判断**，不是上述论文逐项采用了同一对照组合的声称：

1. 最终若选 pEGFP-N1 衍生 TFEB–EGFP，则使用对应未插入 TFEB 的 pEGFP-N1 作为 EGFP-only 对照；若定稿骨架改变，对照同步改变。空载体用于解释标签/表达背景，不证明融合蛋白保留天然功能。
2. Mock-transfected 细胞用于成像与抗体背景；Torin1 使用等终浓度溶剂对照；饥饿组应与相同时间点的营养完整培养组比较。溶剂对照不能替代营养完整对照的定义。
3. anti-GFP WB 关注融合蛋白的主要免疫反应性条带，同时记录额外条带。EGFP-only 对照位置应由其真实翻译序列决定；在融合组看到近 EGFP 位置的条带，只能列为游离 EGFP/蛋白水解等候选解释，不能未经验证断言原因。
4. 理论质量应明确是最终翻译产物的未修饰序列质量，包括接头。WB 用其指导解释，不设虚假的精确迁移阈值。加载量/转膜校验与抗体条件仍由 A 的具体方案定稿；全细胞 WB 不承担定位证明。
5. 课程核心读出保持定位。内源一致性与通量属于解释边界，本文件不把新增功能实验写成本次 B 完成的前置条件。

## 英文正文建议

以下均为自主归纳，可按最终构型和 sequence-ready 数值局部衔接；不是论文原句。引用键对应下一节。

**Background / hypothesis**

TFEB couples nutrient sensing to transcriptional regulation of lysosomal and autophagy-related genes. Nutrient-sensitive phosphorylation and nucleocytoplasmic trafficking regulate its distribution. The proposed experiment tests whether a sequence-verified fluorescent reporter shows a higher nuclear-to-cytoplasmic fluorescence ratio after starvation or mTOR inhibition than under the corresponding control conditions. It is a validation-oriented experimental design, with no experimental outcomes reported here. [Settembre2012; Napolitano2018]

**Fusion rationale — only if the accepted construct is TFEB–EGFP**

A C-terminal EGFP fusion was selected because a TFEB–EGFP reporter has a direct precedent in HeLa nutrient-response studies. N-terminal EGFP fusions have also been reported; the choice does not establish that either orientation is intrinsically superior. The final coding sequence, junctions, and termination strategy are defined by the sequence-verified construct. [RoczniakFerguson2012; Addgene38119; Park2021]

**WB / expected results**

The unmodified theoretical mass will be taken from the verified fusion-protein sequence, including the junction residues. Anti-GFP immunoblotting will assess expression and additional immunoreactive species. Apparent mobility may differ from theoretical mass, and TFEB phosphorylation-dependent mobility shifts have been reported. Additional bands will be documented without assigning their identity solely from apparent size. [RoczniakFerguson2012; Martina2013]

**Limitations**

Tagging and overexpression may alter reporter behavior. A localization endpoint measures net redistribution and does not resolve nuclear import from export. Whole-cell immunoblotting supports expression assessment but does not establish localization, and nuclear enrichment alone does not demonstrate increased autophagic flux. [Napolitano2018; Settembre2012]
